Test the game on your phone

Sting Boost is a 15-second race against a rival. Tap the Boost button at the right moment, three times, and beat him to the line. We need to see how it runs on as many different phones as possible, old ones too.

Start the device test Just play

Takes about a minute. We record the phone name, its screen and graphics chip, and how smoothly the game ran. Nothing about you.

How the test works

  1. Open it on your phone

    Use the button above, or scan the QR code from a computer. Any phone helps; older and cheaper ones help most.

  2. Check your phone’s name

    We fill in a guess. Make sure it says the model and system, for example iPhone 11, iOS 17 or Galaxy A52, Android 13. iPhones never tell us the model, so type it in (Settings › General › About).

  3. Tap AUTO TEST ×3 and let go

    The game races three times by itself, so every phone does the same work. Keep the screen on and don’t touch it for about a minute.

  4. Done. Results send themselves

    When the races finish, the numbers go to us automatically. If sending fails, tap SEND. Then tap PLAY and have a go yourself.

Optional Got a few more minutes? Two extra tests

+1 min

Battery saver

  1. Turn on Low Power Mode (iPhone) or Battery Saver (Android) in your phone’s settings.
  2. On the test screen, tick Battery saver / low power mode is ON.
  3. Tap AUTO TEST ×3 again. Turn the mode off afterwards.

+3 min

Stress ×10

  1. Tap STRESS ×10 on the test screen.
  2. Ten races run back to back. Keep the screen on and don’t touch it.
  3. It shows whether your phone slows down once it gets warm.
